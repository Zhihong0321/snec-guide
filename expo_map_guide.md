# 🍀 SNEC PV Power & Smart Energy Expo: Floor Plan & Venue Layout Guide

Welcome to the definitive Venue & Floor Layout Guide for the **SNEC International Photovoltaic Power Generation & Smart Energy Joint Exhibition** at the **National Exhibition and Convention Center (NECC) in Shanghai**. This guide outlines the spatial layout, floor distributions, transport routing, and entrance wayfinding strategies to maximize efficiency when touring the expo.

---

## 🏛️ Venue Architecture: NECC Shanghai (The Four-Leaf Clover)
The National Exhibition and Convention Center (NECC) Shanghai is the world's largest single building and exhibition complex, shaped like a symmetrical **four-leaf clover**. It consists of 14 massive halls distributed across two levels:
*   **First Floor (1F - Ground Level):** Features the single-digit "H" halls (e.g., `1.1H`, `2.1H`, `3H`, `4.1H`, `5.1H`, `6.1H`, `7.1H`, `8.1H`). These halls have high ceilings and are ideal for heavy equipment and system-level setups.
*   **Second Floor (2F - Elevated Level):** Features the high-level halls (e.g., `1.2H`, `2.2H`, `5.2H`, `6.2H`, `7.2H`, `8.2H`). These halls are located directly above the 1F halls.

## 🚇 Transit & Gate Wayfinding Directions

NECC Shanghai is massive, and entering through the wrong gate can add 30–45 minutes of walking. Use these tailored transit guidelines:

### 1. Arriving by Metro (Recommended)
*   **Metro Line 2 (East Entrance):** Exit at **East Xujing Station (徐泾东站)**. Take **Exit 4 or 5** to arrive directly at the **East Plaza Entrance** (closest to Halls 4.1, 5.1, and Leaf B/C).
*   **Metro Line 17 (West Entrance):** Exit at **Zhugeguang Road Station (诸光路站)**. Walk through the pedestrian bridge directly to the **West Plaza Entrance** (closest to Halls 1.1, 2.1, and Leaf A/D).

### 2. Arriving by Taxi or Ride-Share
*   **North Entrance Drop-off (No. 333 Songze Avenue):** Closest for visitors targeting the production equipment section (Halls 1.1 & 1.2).
*   **South Entrance Drop-off (No. 181 Yinggang East Road):** Closest for visitors targeting the Tier 1 module manufacturers (Halls 7.2 & 8.2) or the battery cells section (Hall 7.1).

### 3. Walking Routes Inside the Venue
*   The **Central Boulevard (8-meter elevated circular corridor)** connects all leaves of the clover. Always use the 2F elevated circular walkway to transition between Leaves A, B, C, and D without having to re-enter security or deal with ground-level logistics trucks.
*   **Leaf-to-Leaf Navigation:**
    *   *Leaf A (Halls 1, 2) ↔ Leaf B (Halls 3, 4):* 5 mins walk via North-East corridor.
    *   *Leaf B (Halls 3, 4) ↔ Leaf C (Halls 5, 6):* 8 mins walk via East-South corridor.
    *   *Leaf C (Halls 5, 6) ↔ Leaf D (Halls 7, 8):* 5 mins walk via South-West corridor.
    *   *Leaf D (Halls 7, 8) ↔ Leaf A (Halls 1, 2):* 8 mins walk via West-North corridor.
